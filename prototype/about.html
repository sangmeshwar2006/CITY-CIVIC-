About City Civic Portal

Empowering citizens to build better Indian cities

🌆 Our Vision

To create cleaner, safer, and smarter Indian cities by enabling citizens to actively participate in reporting and tracking civic issues.

🎯 Our Mission

City Civic Portal provides a simple digital platform where citizens can report civic problems such as road damage, waste management, water issues, sanitation, and public safety concerns.

⚙ How It Works

  • Citizens report civic issues with location details
  • Issues are categorized and displayed publicly
  • Status tracking ensures transparency
  • Authorities can take timely action

🇮🇳 Government Alignment

This platform supports initiatives like Swachh Bharat Abhiyan, Smart Cities Mission, and Digital India by promoting digital governance and citizen engagement.

🚀 Future Scope

  • User login & issue history
  • Admin dashboard for authorities
  • AI-based issue prioritization
  • Multilingual support
